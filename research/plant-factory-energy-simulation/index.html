Plant factory energy simulation · EnergyCEA

A climate-aware energy model for plant factories.

EnergyCEA is a multi-zone dynamic simulation framework that couples a physical-environment model with a calibrated strawberry growth module to quantify the energy-yield relationship in plant factories with artificial lighting.

Plant FactoryVertical FarmingDigital TwinStrawberry ProductionEnergy Optimization

Why this work?

Plant factories with artificial lighting are energy intensive. Standard single-zone models cannot represent the thermal and moisture differences between crop canopies, surrounding air, and the wider room in large-scale vertical production.

Approach

EnergyCEA couples a triple-zone physical environment model with a calibrated strawberry growth module and validates the framework against operational data from a commercial facility.

It evaluates vertical-scaling effects and uses global psychrometric sensitivity analyses to identify how climate, equipment performance, and air enthalpy constrain energy use.

Primary source

Xia, F. et al. (2026). EnergyCEA: A multi-zone dynamic simulation framework to quantify energy determinants and climate sensitivity in plant factories. Applied Energy, 415, 127824.

Questions this work answers

What is EnergyCEA?

It is a multi-zone dynamic simulation framework for quantifying energy determinants and climate sensitivity in plant factories.

Why use multiple zones?

Separate crop-canopy, surrounding-air, and room zones represent thermodynamic heterogeneity that a single well-mixed zone misses.
