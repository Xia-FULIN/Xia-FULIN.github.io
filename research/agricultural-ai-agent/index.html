Agricultural AI agent · ChatCEA

An AI agent grounded in controlled-environment knowledge.

ChatCEA is a knowledge-driven intelligent service agent for controlled environment agriculture that combines large language models with a domain knowledge base, retrieval, and knowledge integration.

AI Agent for AgricultureAgriculture LLMRAGControlled Environment Agriculture

Why this work?

CEA growers work with distributed manuals, scientific papers, quantitative parameters, and evolving production decisions. General search and general-purpose language models often lack the context needed for precise, actionable answers.

Approach

ChatCEA combines a two-source knowledge base built from management manuals and journal papers, a retrieval module for contextual semantic search, and a knowledge-integration module for unstructured and quantitative information.

The work also establishes a CEA-specific benchmark spanning major production subdomains.

Primary source

Xia, F. et al. (2026). ChatCEA: a knowledge-driven intelligent service agent for controlled environment agriculture. Computers and Electronics in Agriculture, 248, 111733.

What does its benchmark measure?
