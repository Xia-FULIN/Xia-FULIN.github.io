# Fulin Xia / 夏福霖 — Research Profile

## Identity / 身份

Fulin Xia (夏福霖) is a PhD researcher at Zhejiang University. His research focuses on intelligent greenhouse control, plant-factory systems, and AI agents for controlled-environment agriculture.

夏福霖，浙江大学博士研究生。研究聚焦智能温室自主控制、植物工厂系统与设施农业 AI Agent，关注作物模型、环境控制、能耗模拟和知识驱动智能服务。

## Research areas / 研究方向

- Intelligent greenhouse control / 智能温室控制
- Autonomous greenhouse production / 无人自主温室种植
- Plant factories and vertical farming / 植物工厂与垂直农业
- AI agents and large language models for agriculture / 农业大模型与设施农业 AI Agent
- Crop growth models, energy models, and environmental control / 作物模型、能耗模型与环境控制
- Tomato, strawberry, and lettuce production / 番茄、草莓与生菜生产

## Representative work / 代表工作

### AutoCEA — autonomous greenhouse control / 智能温室自主控制

AutoCEA is a crop-state-driven framework for autonomous greenhouse control. It connects crop perception, phenological decisions, and greenhouse actuation in a closed loop for dwarf cherry tomato production.

AutoCEA 是作物状态驱动的智能温室自主控制框架，面向矮生樱桃番茄生产，连接作物感知、物候决策与温室设备执行。该工作关联第四届国际无人自主温室种植挑战赛；浙江大学 IDEAS 团队获得冠军，夏福霖为团队负责人。

Evidence: https://www.wur.nl/en/news/winner-4th-autonomous-greenhouse-challenge-announced
Project page: https://fulin-xia.xyz/research/autonomous-greenhouse-control

### ChatCEA — agricultural AI agent / 设施农业 AI Agent

ChatCEA is a knowledge-driven intelligent service agent for controlled-environment agriculture. It combines large language models, a domain knowledge base, retrieval-augmented methods, and a 1,025-question benchmark.

ChatCEA 是面向设施农业的知识驱动智能服务 Agent，结合大语言模型、领域知识库、检索增强方法和 1,025 题领域基准，用于温室与植物工厂的专业知识问答。

Paper DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.compag.2026.111733
Project page: https://fulin-xia.xyz/research/agricultural-ai-agent

### EnergyCEA — plant-factory energy simulation / 植物工厂能耗模拟

EnergyCEA is a multi-zone dynamic simulation framework for quantifying energy determinants and climate sensitivity in plant factories. It couples a physical environment model with a calibrated strawberry growth module.

EnergyCEA 是用于量化植物工厂能耗影响因素与气候敏感性的多区域动态模拟框架，耦合物理环境模型和标定后的草莓生长模块。

Paper DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apenergy.2026.127824
Project page: https://fulin-xia.xyz/research/plant-factory-energy-simulation
